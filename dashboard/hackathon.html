Four moves. Zero decisions for you.

Timo never hands you a to-do list. It brings you one pick, already lined up.

1

Watches

It reads your calendar for free gaps and works out when you need to leave, from how you get around.

2

Decides

It picks one thing that fits the time: the task due soonest, something you can do from a phone, or a reply to send.

3

Acts

It lines up the safe, reversible steps itself and has the draft ready before it reaches you.

4

Reaches out

It calls when you're about to leave and texts the rest of the time. Say yes on the call and it acts while you're still on the line.

Why Timo is different

It calls you first.

Timo decides when to reach out: in a free gap, or 20 minutes before you need to leave. It also knows when to stay quiet.

SMS is the control plane.

One-word replies steer the agent, and a single Y approves an action. There are no menus or screens to learn.

A real phone number.

Timo calls and texts over Dial. A spoken yes can run a tool during the call, and every action that affects someone else is confirmed first.

Safe by design.

Rules live in code: text versus call, quiet hours, at most two calls a day. Anything that touches other people waits for a yes.

Still works if the model is down.

Timo still sends the leave-by time. An unanswered call becomes a text. Webhooks are signed and deduplicated.

Hands-free when it counts.

Biking, driving or on the bus, voice gets you the plan without touching a screen.

Dial is the spine.

Every part of Timo runs through Dial's phone layer. It isn't one API call bolted on; it's the whole interface.
